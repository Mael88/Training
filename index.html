Mesocycle 6 — Rebuild 2 Upper · 2 Legs · 2 Bag

Aim: rebuild the upper/leg balance after Mesocycle 5 ended early to gastroenteritis, and after a cervical flare-up linked to suspected upper body overtraining (fully resolved after one week of rest). Split moves from 3 upper + 1 legs to 2 upper + 2 legs, redistributing weekly load away from the neck and shoulders.

Bag restarts near Week 3 intensity from Mesocycle 5 — the technical toolkit (full punches, kicks, knees, hook-knee flow, atemi tactical framing) is already established. This is a load and volume rebuild, not a technique rebuild.

  • Upper intensity capped — 2–3 reps from failure, no max sets, through Week 3
  • No explosive pulling — muscle-up prep phase only continues if still in that phase
  • Kick volume moderate — first 2 weeks, while legs adapt to doubled frequency (now trained Tue + Fri)
  • Rear mawashi geri recovery correction — ongoing, multi-week motor pattern fix
  • Lead mawashi hip strength — ongoing 3×/week hip protocol, patience expected
  • Shoulder depression — master cue on every punch and hook, every round

Stop Criteria · non-negotiable
Headache during or right after a session, or a cervical flare-up → cut the week and rest. No mid-week negotiation.
Arm pain, numbness, tingling or weakness below the shoulder → stop training and get assessed. Nerve territory, not muscle tension.
Headaches persisting past 4 weeks despite desk changes and daily prehab → book physio for the muscular driver.
