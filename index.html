Where I Am Right Now
Week 7 — Re-entry Post-illness

Aim: re-expose the body after two weeks off (week 6 lost to gastroenteritis). This is not a deload — enough rest has already happened. Three light sessions, everything well short of failure, then straight into the new mesocycle.

This week only: 1 technical bag session (RPE 5, Z2, ~30 min, no output rounds) + 1 light upper + 1 light legs. If energy is still low, drop the bag session.

  • Upper intensity capped — 2–3 reps from failure, no max sets, through week 3 of the new cycle
  • No explosive pulling — muscle-up prep phase only (false grip, scapular pulls, ring support, assisted transitions)
  • Kick volume moderate — first 2 weeks, while the legs adapt to doubled frequency
  • Shoulder depression — master cue on every punch, every round

Every day — including rest days
Cervical prehab ~5 min · DNF Stage 2 craniocervical holds, building to 10×10 sec · Desk micro-breaks every 30–45 min (chin tucks + shoulder rolls) · second prehab dose on heavy desk days.
Why it's laid out this way: upper and legs each sit three days apart, and the hardest day (Bag B) falls right before the rest day. The compromise is Friday legs into Saturday Bag B — that's why kick volume stays moderate early in the cycle.

Hip blocks (3×12 min) go on the upper days and Sunday, not the leg days: legs are fresh so the work is high quality, and the 45-minute cap on Tuesday and Friday stays intact. Sunday's block can move to Wednesday before Bag A instead, used as a kicking-specific warm-up.

Stop Criteria · non-negotiable
Headache during or right after a session, or a cervical flare-up → cut the week and rest. No mid-week negotiation.
Arm pain, numbness, tingling or weakness below the shoulder → stop training and get assessed. Nerve territory, not muscle tension.
Headaches persisting past 4 weeks despite desk changes and daily prehab → book physio for the muscular driver.
Log after every session: cervical status · evening headache · desk hours · prehab done.
The correlation that matters is headache vs desk hours, not headache vs training.
