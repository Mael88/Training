Mawashi Geri — Shin vs Foot Distance Calibration
NEW FOCUS
Reading Distance Deliberately
Shin range: stand at your normal working distance. Throw a rear mawashi geri aiming to land mid-shin on the bag. This is the more forgiving, more powerful, lower-injury-risk option — your default when in doubt.
Foot range: step back roughly half a step from shin range. Throw the same kick, but now aim to land with the top of the foot or ball of foot, ankle extended. This requires the leg to fully extend to reach — if you're still at shin distance, you'll jam the shin into the bag instead of landing clean with the foot.
The skill: before committing to power, quickly read which range you're actually at. Shin range = commit with shin. Foot range = extend and land with the foot. Don't guess — check your distance first.
Shin range rear mawashi ×3 → step back half a step → foot range rear mawashi ×3 → step back in → repeat, both legs.

Shin vs Foot — New This Week
  • Shin range: closer distance, forgiving on timing, lower injury risk, strong power. Default choice when in doubt or inside a combination.
  • Foot range: roughly half a step further, needs the leg to fully extend, ankle pointed. Higher injury risk if timing is off — too close and the shin jams into the target instead.
  • The skill: read your distance before committing to power. Don't default to one or the other by accident.
