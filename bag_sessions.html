Shin vs Foot — Clarification Needed
The skill being trained is distance, not height. Attempting head-height kicks during foot-range reps mixes two different skills — distance-reading and height progression — making it hard to isolate either one. Your instinct that foot contact correlates with higher kicks has some truth to it (trajectory does shift that way), but this week both shin and foot reps stay at the same body-height target. Head-height kicking is a real, separate goal tied to the ongoing hip mobility work — a longer-term thread for a future cycle, not something to chase now, especially in taper.

Body Height Only — Pure Distance Read
