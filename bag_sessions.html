New 14oz Gloves — Expect an Adjustment Period
Heavier gloves add real rotational resistance to every punch — your arm is moving more mass on each strike than it was on 10–12oz. This fatigues the shoulder and arm faster, especially by the back half of a session, and is exactly what you felt last week. It is not a technique regression. Lean harder into the shoulder blade depression cue this week — it matters even more with added glove weight, since a lazy shoulder now has more mass to compensate for with the arm alone. Expect this to feel normal again within 1–2 sessions.
Same targets as Week 1. Confirm the HR chest strap connects before starting — last week's data came from an ankle-placed optical sensor and wasn't reliable.
