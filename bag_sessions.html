Heart Rate — Fix The Strap Before Wednesday
Saturday’s 177 came from the ankle sensor, so it doesn’t count. Before the session: wet the electrode pads, wear the strap snug just under the chest muscles, and check the watch is showing strap HR before you start the warm-up. Warm-Up

Cervical Prehab 6 min

  • Chin tucks — 2×10, head slides back, gaze level
  • Cervical rotations — 5 reps each side, pain-free only
  • Lateral neck stretch — 20–30 sec each side, gravity only
  • Scapular retractions — 2×15
  • Shrug and drop — shrug 3 sec, release fully, ×8
  • Shoulder depression drill — 10 reps, hold 3 sec
  • Wall slides, shoulder blades down — 1×10, stop where the shoulders shrug

Hip & Adductor Mobility 4 min

  • 10 wide hip circles each side
  • Dynamic lateral lunges — 8 each side, build range gradually
  • Dynamic chamber openers — 8 each side, no held positions

Movement Prep 2 min

  • Light shadowboxing — all weapons, no power
  • 10 knee raises each side
  • 30 sec light bounce
Main Work — 5 Rounds
3 min rounds · 90 sec rest · RPE 6 · At RPE 9 the remaining rounds drop to recovery pace. At 30 °C or hotter, rest 75 sec.
1
Full Punch Toolkit
Jab, cross, both hooks, all combinations. 60% effort. Two weeks off the bag: use this round to find timing, wrist position and the glove weight again. Shoulder blade depressed on every punch.
2
Distance Ladder Step 1 — Stand, Call, Kick
NEW LADDER
Same Body Height — Say It Out Loud
Target: rib/body height for both. Shin range: stand at normal distance, land mid-shin. Foot range: half a step further back, land with the top of the foot, ankle extended, toes pointed down.
Say “shin” or “foot” before the kick, then land on that point. Lead leg first and longer — it is the one lagging behind the rear.
Lead: shin ×3 → step back → foot ×3 → step in → repeat (about 1 min). Rear: same (about 1 min). Last minute = blind calls, see below.
Blind calls (last minute): settle into a distance without looking down, call it, kick, then check where it landed. Keep a running tally — matched or not — and log it after the session.
3
Rear Mawashi + Cross-Lead Hook + Rear Hiza Geri
Rear mawashi at shin range (the default) → pause → step off the swing line → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow). Sharp and clean at 60–65%, not maximal. Kick volume moderate this week: 2–3 clean reps of the sequence per minute, and reset between.
Rear mawashi (shin range) → pause → step off line → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow) → step back → reset.
Check the chamber return after every kick: knee pulls back before the foot goes down.
4
Lead Mawashi + Jab-Cross + Rear Hiza Geri
Lead mawashi (shin range) → pause → step in → jab-cross → rear hiza geri (flow). Log the hip drive: does the hip lead, is the torso neutral, do you pause after landing.
Lead mawashi → pause → step in → jab-cross → rear hiza geri (flow) → step back → reset.
5
Free Round — Full Toolkit, Body-Height Kicks
No prescribed combination. RPE 6 ceiling. Full toolkit, body-height kicks only. Finish with 3 distance calls (call → kick → check) before the bell — that is your closing rep for the week.
Cool-Down

Upper Body & Traps 4 min

  • Chest & shoulder stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Wrist flexor + extensor stretch — 20 sec each side
  • Upper trap stretch — sit on the hand of the side you stretch, ear toward the opposite shoulder by gravity, 30 sec each side
  • Levator stretch — nose toward the opposite armpit, gravity only, 30 sec each side

Lower Body & Breathing 4 min

  • Hip flexor lunge — 45 sec each side
  • Standing quad stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Adductor stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Lateral hip rotator stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Breathing reset lying down — 2 min, 4 sec in through the belly / 6 sec out, shoulders still

Post-Session Log

Coaching Notes

The call-before-kick is the whole point of the week. Saying the distance out loud forces the read to happen before the leg moves, which is where the skill lives. Accuracy matters more than the number of kicks.

If both legs feel heavy in Round 1, keep the kick rounds at the lower end and let the punches carry the work. No need to prove anything in week one.

Mistakes & Cues

Carried from Mesocycle 6
Standing corrections, plus the new distance-reading mistakes to watch for. Keep this handy through the cycle.
Strike Cues
StrikeKey cue
Jab / Cross / HooksShoulder blade down, elbow close, hip snaps first
Rear MawashiControlled chamber return (knee back before foot down), heel pivots outward
Lead MawashiHip leads, torso neutral, pause after landing — the side still catching up
Hiza GeriVertical drive, flow from hook, no pause
Both MawashiRead shin vs foot distance before committing
Distance Reading — Common Mistakes
MistakeFix
Foot-range kick lands with the toesExtend the ankle and point the toes down so the top of the foot makes contact
Leaning back to reach foot rangeStep back half a step instead; the torso stays neutral
Kick height drops or rises with the distanceSame body-height target for shin and foot. This is a distance drill only
Calling after the leg is already movingSay it first, then kick. The read must happen before the hip turns
Chasing kick countAccuracy matters more than volume, especially in Week 1

  • Pause after kicks, flow after hooks and knees
