Before You Start
Headaches — Stop Rule
A headache during or right after the session means stop and cut the rest of the week. That is the standing stop rule. Any arm pain, numbness or tingling: stop and get assessed.
Cold — Above-The-Neck Rule
Residual blocked nose or mild throat only: go ahead at RPE 5. Anything below the neck (chest, productive cough, fever, aching body): skip and rest instead.
Six Weeks Off The Bag
Timing will feel off, the 14oz gloves will feel heavy, wrists and hips will need a round. That is normal and not a sign the session should be harder. Chest strap on.
One bag session this week, by design. Wednesday becomes your make-up legs day, so the bag waits until Saturday. The aim is simple: reconnect with the bag and find out how the neck and traps respond. Next week is Week 1 of Mesocycle 7.
Warm-Up

Cervical Prehab 5 min

  • Chin tucks — 2×10, head slides back, gaze level
  • Cervical rotations — 5 reps each side, pain-free only
  • Lateral neck stretch — 20–30 sec each side, gravity only
  • Scapular retractions — 2×15
  • Shrug and drop — shrug 3 sec, release fully, ×8
  • Shoulder depression drill — 10 reps, hold 3 sec

Hip & Adductor Mobility 4 min

  • 10 wide hip circles each side
  • Dynamic lateral lunges — 8 each side, build range gradually
  • Dynamic chamber openers — 8 each side, no held positions

Movement Prep 2 min

  • Light shadowboxing — all weapons, no power
  • 10 knee raises each side
  • 30 sec light bounce
Main Work — 4 Rounds
3 min rounds · 90 sec rest · RPE 5 · If HR passes 145 bpm, lower the pace. No output work this week.
1
Jab & Cross Only
50% effort. Find the distance and the timing again. Shoulder blade pulled down on every punch, exhale on every strike.
Trap check: between combinations, let both shoulders drop. If they creep toward the ears, slow down — that creep is the tension you are trying to get rid of.
2
Hooks + Hook → Knee Flow
Both hooks from the hip, elbow close, 50%. Then the established flow: cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri, no pause. Light knees — this is about the pattern, not the impact.
Jab → cross → lead hook ×3 → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow) → reset.
3
Kick Mechanics — Low Volume
Body height only, shin range only. Lead side first. Roughly 10 kicks per leg in the whole round — quality, not count.
Lead mae geri ×3 → rear mae geri ×3 → lead mawashi ×3 → rear mawashi ×3 → easy bounce, repeat once.
Check the controlled chamber return on every mawashi: knee back before the foot goes down. If the adductors feel tight, shorten the range rather than forcing it.
4
Easy Flow — Full Toolkit
Everything you have, at 50–60%, close to moving shadowboxing with contact. Finish this round feeling you could easily do three more. That feeling is the target for the whole session.
Cool-Down

Upper Body & Traps 4 min

  • Chest & shoulder stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Upper trap stretch — sit on the hand of the side you stretch, ear toward the opposite shoulder by gravity, 30 sec each side
  • Levator stretch — nose toward the opposite armpit, gravity only, 30 sec each side

Lower Body & Breathing 4 min

  • Hip flexor lunge — 45 sec each side
  • Adductor stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Lateral hip rotator stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Breathing reset lying down — 2 min, 4 sec in through the belly / 6 sec out, shoulders still

Trap Tension Plan

Evening ~5 minNo timersTrack headaches 2–3 wks
What you’re fighting
Not lactic acid — that clears within about an hour. It’s muscle tone that builds up from training, desk and theatre posture and stress, then drives headaches later in the day. The plan below attacks it with as little new habit as possible: fix the desk once, release the muscle in the evening attached to something you already do, and stop training from adding to it.
1 · Once — Fix The Desk
Works all day without you doing anything. Probably the biggest lever.
1
Forearms supported
Rest your forearms on the desk or on armrests at elbow height while typing, reading or on the mouse. Arms hanging or reaching forward keeps the upper traps switched on all day.
2
Screen at eye level
Top of the screen at or slightly below eye level, about an arm’s length away. A laptop needs a stand plus a separate keyboard.
3
Keyboard and mouse close
Elbows by your sides, wrists straight, hands at or just below elbow level.
4
Phone and calls
Headset for long calls. Bring the phone up to your eyes instead of dropping your head to it.
Mayo Clinic: office ergonomics guide
2 · Every Evening — Attached To Your Shower
Same order every night so it runs on autopilot. Shower first, the rest straight after.
1
Heat2–3 min
In the shower, hot water on the upper traps. Or a heat pack for 15 min on the sofa instead.
2
Massage gun or ball1–2 min per side
Gun: low setting, slow sweeps over the meaty part of the upper trap, 10–15 sec max on a tender spot. Ball: tennis or lacrosse ball between the upper trap and a wall, lean in to about 5/10 pressure and breathe. For both: stay off the spine, the bony points and the front and side of the neck.

3 · In Training — Don’t Add To It
  • Pull-ups and dips: when the shoulders start shrugging toward the ears, the set is over. Grinding reps with the traps is where much of the training tension comes from.
  • Bag: shoulder blades down on every punch; drop the shoulders between combinations.
  • Never skip the cool-down trap stretches and breathing — it’s the one built-in moment of the day for this.
4 · 2–3× Per Week — In Your Warm-Up

1

5 · Triggers, Not Timers
  • Tie one shrug-and-drop plus one slow breath to things that already happen: finishing a case, closing a patient file, sitting back down at the desk. Ten seconds, no break needed.
  • Backstop: Garmin’s Move Alert can buzz you after long sitting.
6 · The Nap — When The Office Is Ready
  • 10–20 min after lunch helps stress and sleep debt, which both feed muscle tone.
  • Lie flat, or recline with the head supported. Sleeping slumped with the head dropping forward loads the neck instead of unloading it.
  • Days without a nap: even 5 min flat on your back with belly breathing takes the head’s weight off the traps.
How We Judge It
Log headache frequency for the next 2–3 weeks. If they keep coming for more than 4 weeks despite this, book a physio for the muscular driver. Any arm symptoms: stop training and get assessed.
