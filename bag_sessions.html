Mae Geri Entry — Two Valid Endings
CHOOSE
Both Patterns Officially Valid — Alternate Deliberately
Pattern A — Controlling finish: mae geri → pause → step in → jab-cross-lead hook → hold clinch 1 sec → step back. Ends in a held position, no knee. Tactically: you have entered and controlled, ready to disengage or continue from a position of dominance.
Pattern B — Finishing strike: mae geri → pause → step in → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow, drop the jab) → step back. Ends in a strike, not a hold. Tactically: you have entered and struck decisively rather than pausing to control.
Alternate between the two this round — 3 reps of Pattern A, then 3 reps of Pattern B. Both are correct. The choice in a real scenario would depend on what you want to do next: control or finish.
