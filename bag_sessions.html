Week 3 Debrief — Diagnosis Corrected
Not Supporting-Leg Strength — Adductor/Hip Mobility
Good re-diagnosis. Holding the kicking leg chambered (hip flexed, externally rotated) puts real stretch load on the adductors. Limited mobility in that range, especially cold, produces exactly what you described — harder at the start, easing as tissue warms. This is a flexibility/warm-up issue, not a strength ceiling. Last week’s calf raises and single-leg balance work weren’t targeting the actual problem — replaced this week with proper adductor and hip-opening mobility.
Monitor — “Slightly Painful” Needs a Clear Line
Discomfort that eases with warmth is normal tightness. If it’s ever sharp, doesn’t ease as you warm up, or lingers after the session, that's a different signal — back off kicks that day and reassess rather than pushing through. Keep this self-check running every session.

Mawashi geri overall described as “clearly improved a lot” since starting this work. Legs fresh both sessions. Rear mawashi recovery holding mostly automatic. Full kick volume landing well. Week 4 moves into Intensification — the rebuild phase is complete.

Adductor / Hip Mobility — Corrected Diagnosis
  • Not this: supporting leg strength or stability during rotation.
  • Actually this: the chambered kicking-leg position (hip flexed + externally rotated) stretches the adductors. Limited mobility in that range, especially cold, causes discomfort that eases as tissue warms.
  • Fix: dynamic adductor mobility in warm-up (lateral lunges, dynamic chamber openers), gradual range build-up before full-intensity kicks, no deliberately held chamber positions.
  • Watch for: normal tightness eases with warmth. Sharp pain or discomfort that doesn't ease is a different signal — back off and reassess.
