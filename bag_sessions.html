1
Full Punch Toolkit

2
Rear Mawashi Recovery — Continued
ONGOING
Same Correction, Moderate Volume
Rear mawashi geri, slow tempo, alternating with lead mawashi for contrast. After impact, pull the knee back to chamber before placing the foot down. Fewer total reps than a peak-volume week — quality over quantity given legs are also handling a new Tuesday legs session two days prior.
Lead mawashi (natural recovery) → rear mawashi (deliberate chamber return) → repeat, moderate total volume.
3
Hook-Knee Flow + Mae Geri Check
Lead hook → rear hiza geri, flowing with no pause. Then a few mae geri reps to confirm chamber and hip drive. 65% effort, moderate total kick count.
Lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow) → release → step back → reset.
4
Rear Mawashi + Cross-Lead Hook + Rear Hiza Geri
Your sharpest combination. Rear mawashi with controlled recovery → pause → step off swing line → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow). 65–70% effort, moderate rep count on the kick portion.
Rear mawashi (controlled recovery) → pause → step off line → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri (flow) → step back → reset.
5
Free Round — Full Toolkit, Moderate Kicks
No prescribed combination. Full toolkit available, but lean toward punches and knees over kicks this round — a natural way to keep kick volume moderate without rigid counting.
Legs will tell you if the new frequency is too much this week. Listen to that signal.
