Re-Entry Week · Post-Illness

Bag Session

~30 min RPE 5 Z2 · 114–133 bpm One session only
Re-Entry After Illness — Different From a Normal Re-Entry
Gastroenteritis, not a training break. Week 6 was lost entirely — no bag, no calisthenics. This is not a deload; it is a cautious return. One technical bag session, no output work, RPE 5 hard ceiling. If energy is still low when you start, stop and skip it entirely — there is no obligation to complete this session.
Cervical — Fully Resolved
Confirmed resolved. No cervical modifications needed today beyond the standard prehab routine. This gives genuine confidence to proceed with the bag session as planned.
What Comes Next
Mesocycle 6 begins on a new weekly split — 2 upper + 2 legs + 2 bag, replacing the 3 upper + 1 legs template. Reason: suspected upper body overtraining contributed to the cervical flare-up that preceded this illness. Bag work restarts around Week 3 intensity level, not from zero — the technical foundation from Mesocycle 5 is still there.
Technique only, no output. This single session checks that the toolkit is intact after two weeks away from the bag (one week illness, prior week already reduced). Nothing pushed, nothing tested.
Warm-Up

Cervical Prehab 5 min

  • Chin tucks — 2×10, head slides back, gaze level
  • Cervical rotations — 5 reps each side, pain-free only
  • Lateral neck stretch — 20–30 sec each side, gravity only
  • Scapular retractions — 2×15
  • Shoulder rolls — 10 each direction
  • Shoulder depression drill — 10 reps, hold 3 sec

Movement Prep 3 min

  • Light shadowboxing — no power, just find rhythm and stance
  • 10 hip circles each side, slow
  • 10 knee raises each side
  • 30 sec very light bounce
Main Work — 4 Rounds
3 min rounds · 90 sec rest · RPE 5 · No output rounds. Mechanics only.
1
Punches — Slow Mechanics Check
Jab, cross, both hooks. 40–45% effort, deliberately slow. This round is purely a check — does the shoulder blade cue come back automatically, does everything feel structurally normal.
2
Kicks — Slow Mechanics Check
Mae geri, lead mawashi, rear mawashi — slow, 40–45% effort. Confirm chamber, pivot, and the controlled recovery on the rear mawashi. Nothing pushed.
If legs feel notably weaker than expected after two weeks away, that is normal — not a sign to push harder to compensate.
3
Knees — Slow Mechanics Check
Hiza geri with bag grip, slow and controlled. 40–45% effort. Confirm the vertical drive is still there.
4
One Familiar Combination — Very Light
Rear mawashi → cross-lead hook → rear hiza geri — your most natural sequence from Mesocycle 5. 45% effort, slow. This is a confidence check, not a test.
If this feels good, that is all the information needed today. Stop here if energy is already dropping — do not add rounds to compensate for a short session.
Cool-Down

Recovery 4 min

  • Chest & shoulder stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Hip flexor lunge — 45 sec each side
  • Upper trap stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Breathing reset — 2 min, slow nasal breathing

Light Upper & Light Legs

CalisthenicsApp content Well short of failure
Programming Boundary
Individual set/rep content for calisthenics belongs to CalisthenicsApp, not here. This page covers the macro instruction only: what “light” means this week and why.
This Week’s Instruction

1 Light Upper Session

  • Use CalisthenicsApp’s prescribed exercises, but stop every set 4–5 reps short of failure — well short, not close.
  • If the app calls for ring work at a demanding progression, use an easier variation this week.
  • No skill work (muscle-up progressions continue only if the app’s content stays in the preparatory phase — no explosive pulling this week regardless).
  • Full cervical prehab before and after.

1 Light Legs Session

  • Same principle — CalisthenicsApp content, stopped well short of failure.
  • This is also the session to reintroduce hip rotator exercises (clamshells, side-lying abduction, fire hydrants, standing hip circles) at the usual light protocol — 30 sec between sets, 60 sec between exercises.
Why Only 1 Each This Week
Two weeks without structured training (illness plus the reduced week before it) means the body needs a genuine re-entry, not a jump back to normal volume. Mesocycle 6’s full 2+2+2 split begins the following week, once this light week confirms energy and cervical status are both stable.
