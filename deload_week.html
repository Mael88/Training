Mesocycle 4 → 5 · Deload Week

Active Recovery

4–10 May 2026 · 5 active days · 2 rest days
What this week is for

The adaptation from Mesocycle 4 consolidates during this week, not during the training. Your job is to reduce accumulated fatigue while keeping movement patterns warm. Easy walking promotes metabolic clearance. Light mobility work maintains the range of motion you built with kicks. Very light bodyweight movement keeps the neural patterns from going cold. Nothing in this week should feel like training.

Non-Negotiable Rules
No bag work. No bag contact of any kind this week. Shadowboxing is allowed once — 10 minutes maximum, no power, patterns only.
No rings, no loading. The shoulders and connective tissue need genuine rest from loaded pulling and pushing. Bodyweight only.
No pushing effort. Every activity this week should stay well below any breathing effort. If you are breathing hard, you are going too hard. Heart rate should stay in Z1–low Z2 (below 130 bpm) for all activities.
Sleep and eat well. The deload is where the adaptation happens. Extra sleep (even 30 minutes more per night) and consistent Mediterranean nutrition are as important as the movement this week.

Nutrition This Week
Simple adjustments — no strict protocol

Training volume is down ~80% this week so energy expenditure drops. You do not need to drastically reduce food — but be aware that appetite may not drop as fast as expenditure, which can lead to slight excess. A few practical adjustments:

Keep protein high: protein is the main driver of adaptation consolidation. Aim for the same protein intake as a normal training week — fish, legumes, eggs, Greek yoghurt.

Reduce portion size slightly on non-active days (Thursday and Sunday) — no complex calculation, just eat until satisfied rather than full.

Hydrate well: recovery and adaptation are both enhanced by good hydration. Water and herbal tea — reduce coffee if sleep is the priority.

Anti-inflammatory foods: Mediterranean staples — olive oil, oily fish, berries, walnuts, leafy greens — actively support the recovery process. Nothing exotic, just the base of what you already eat.

Sleep Priority
Sleep is the most important recovery tool this week. If you can add 30 minutes per night — earlier bedtime, no screen 30 min before sleep — do it. Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep, which is when structural adaptation consolidates. The training created the signal. Sleep is where the response happens.
